## Q1 — What is BTY

BTY is not a motivation platform. It is a **Deterministic Leadership & Behavioral Alignment Engine**.

BTY does not exist to change how people think. It exists to generate action.

> **BTY does not record action. It verifies the change in choice that follows it.**

---

## Q2 — Why BTY Exists

As an organization grows, its leaders cannot be everywhere at once. From this single limit, four failures emerge together — Cultural Drift (standards erode), Burnout (leaders exhaust themselves correcting), Inconsistency (subjective enforcement), and Reactive Cycles (intervention only after failure).

The root problem is the **Personality Bottleneck**: culture cannot scale if it depends entirely on the charisma and presence of individual leaders.

BTY rests on a single premise:

> **This is not a motivation problem. It is a systems problem.**

So BTY does not correct people. It is built so the structure blocks action before it drifts from the standard.

---

## Q3 — How BTY Works

> **Action is not the goal. Change is the goal.**

Choice is not growth. Action is not change. A person can act rightly once and still collapse under the same pressure again. So BTY does not stop at action. It applies the same pressure again. Has the choice held, or changed? The loop does not close until change is confirmed.

**Re-exposure is the center of gravity of BTY.**

That structure is the 7-Step:

Pressure → Choice → Pattern → Action → Verification → Re-exposure → Change

Placed in a situation under pressure — no right answer, every option plausible — the user makes a choice. Choices accumulate into a pattern, and the pattern forces an action (an Action Contract) that steps into the reality the user has been avoiding.

The action is verified by QR — and until it is complete, all further progress is locked:

> **No Action → No Progression.**

Only after the verified action does BTY re-expose the same pressure, and only once the change in choice is confirmed does the loop close.

## Q4 — What BTY Measures

BTY does not measure knowledge. It measures action. The core metric for that is **AIR**.

> **Action Integrity** = the ability to carry out the right action you committed to, without avoiding it, even under pressure and stress.

Integrity descends into a measurable form:

Self-honesty → Action Integrity → AIR
(philosophy) → (behavioral standard) → (metric)

- **AIR (Action Integrity Rate)** = actions completed / actions chosen. The system's primary metric.
- Raw data stays private. To prevent toxic competition between individuals, status is shown to users only as a band (low / mid / high) and as narrative.

Higher-order indices (LRI, TII, Certified) and growth metrics (Core XP, Weekly XP) all derive from AIR.

They are appendices.

**The body is AIR alone.**

---

## Q5 — What Kind of Leader

Two different questions have two different answers.

**Is this a leader?**
> A leader is **Decision + Action Completion**. In this system, a leader who only thinks cannot exist.

**What kind of leader?**
> The greater the influence, the more they take responsibility rather than credit.
>
> Power is weight, not privilege.
>
> **The more power they hold, the smaller they make themselves.**

The Definition is the gate of qualification; the Ideal is the direction beyond it. BTY first makes a person complete the action (Definition), then slowly aligns them to make themselves smaller before power (Ideal). There is no fast promotion. Time is the filter.
