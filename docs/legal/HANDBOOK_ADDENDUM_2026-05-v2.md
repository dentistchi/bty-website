## 1. Purpose and Scope

### 1.1 What btyARENA Is

btyARENA is an internal leadership and behavioral training platform provided by bty DENTAL (the "Practice") for the purpose of professional development, leadership learning, and organizational growth. The platform's name reflects its underlying philosophy — *Better Than Yesterday* — and is centered on incremental, evidence-based behavioral improvement over time.

btyARENA presents employees with realistic workplace scenarios and supports a structured loop of reflection, action, and behavior verification.

btyARENA consists of three primary environments:

- **Arena** — scenario-based behavioral training and execution practice
- **Foundry** — guided learning and skill development
- **Center** — calibration, recovery, and reflective practice

### 1.2 What This Addendum Covers

This addendum sets forth the Practice's expectations and commitments regarding employee use of btyARENA, including participation, training records, visibility and access controls, and the role btyARENA plays in organizational decision-making. This addendum supplements — and does not replace — the bty DENTAL Employee Handbook. Where this addendum is silent, the Employee Handbook governs.

### 1.3 Who This Applies To

This addendum applies to every bty DENTAL employee who is provided access to btyARENA by the Practice. Use of btyARENA is conditional on the employee's acknowledgment of this addendum.

## 2. Participation Expectations

### 2.1 Work Activity Classification

Time spent on btyARENA scenarios assigned, recommended, or designated by the Practice during scheduled work hours is considered compensable work activity under applicable wage and hour law. Where btyARENA participation is voluntary and conducted outside scheduled work hours, it is not compensable except where otherwise required by law.

### 2.2 Expected Cadence

The Practice may communicate recommended or expected participation cadence (for example, weekly scenario completion). Specific cadence expectations will be communicated separately from this addendum and may evolve as the platform matures.

### 2.3 Voluntary and Required Activities

The Practice distinguishes between:

- **Required activities** — explicitly designated as required by the Practice, with reasonable accommodation available
- **Recommended activities** — encouraged but not required
- **Voluntary activities** — available to employees at their discretion

Non-participation in voluntary activities does not constitute a performance concern.

### 2.4 Accommodation

Employees who require accommodation regarding btyARENA participation — including for disability, religious observance, scheduling, technology access, or any other protected reason — may request accommodation through normal Practice channels. Requests will be addressed in accordance with applicable law and Practice policy.

## 3. Training Records, Visibility, and the Role of btyARENA in Organizational Decisions

This section sets forth the most important commitments in this addendum. Employees are encouraged to read it carefully.

### 3.1 What btyARENA Measures

btyARENA captures behavioral training data generated by employees as they engage with platform scenarios. This data may include scenario choices, reflection responses, action records, verification outcomes, and aggregate readiness or consistency signals over time.

btyARENA does not collect, store, or process protected health information (PHI) of patients. Employees are prohibited from entering any patient information, patient identifiers, or other PHI into btyARENA scenarios, reflections, or any other btyARENA surface.

### 3.2 The Three-Layer Role of btyARENA

The Practice maintains a clear separation between what btyARENA does, what administrators do with btyARENA information, and how the Practice makes decisions about employees:

**Layer 1 — Measurement.** btyARENA measures behavioral patterns, consistency, execution integrity, recovery, and leadership-behavior evidence generated through employee engagement with the platform. This is the function of the system.

**Layer 2 — Evidence.** Designated administrators may use btyARENA information as one source of evidence to support coaching, succession discussion, leadership development, and talent conversations. btyARENA information is reference material, not a verdict.

**Layer 3 — Decision.** All employment decisions — including but not limited to promotion, compensation, discipline, hiring, role assignment, and termination — are made by the Practice through normal human processes that weigh many factors, including job performance, conduct, business need, judgment, and circumstances beyond what any single system can capture.

### 3.3 btyARENA Is Not a Sole or Automatic Decision Engine

**btyARENA provides behavioral leadership analytics and developmental evidence intended to support leadership development and organizational learning. btyARENA metrics are one source of information and are not designed to function as a sole or automatic determinant of promotion, compensation, disciplinary action, or employment decisions.**

The Practice does not promote, demote, discipline, compensate, or terminate employees based on a single btyARENA score, metric, or system output. Leadership selection, performance evaluation, and disciplinary processes involve human judgment of behavior, trust, relationships, operational outcomes, cultural fit, timing, and organizational context — none of which are reducible to a platform output.

### 3.4 Visibility Layers and Administrative Access

btyARENA uses role-based visibility and access controls designed to balance organizational learning, leadership development, and participant experience. Access is structured in three layers:

**Participant Layer.** Participants primarily interact with btyARENA through code names, training progression, and limited developmental feedback designed to support learning and reduce unhealthy comparison. Participant-facing experiences may include:

- XP and progression surfaces
- Tier or code-based identity
- Narrative or trend-based feedback
- Platform participation history

Certain internal behavioral analytics and administrative metrics are not displayed directly to participants.

**Manager Layer.** Managers or designated local leaders may access team-level or group-level information intended to support training participation, team development, and group engagement. Manager access may include:

- Team or office-level participation information
- Aggregate or grouped developmental trends
- Team competition or code-name leaderboard surfaces
- Authorized team-development, QR, or behavioral reinforcement workflows associated with btyARENA participation, subject to Practice policy and role-based permissions

Managers are not provided unrestricted access to identified individual behavioral analytics solely by virtue of supervisory status.

**Enterprise or System Administrator Layer.** Authorized enterprise or system administrators may access identified btyARENA information where reasonably necessary for platform administration, leadership development, organizational learning, coaching support, succession planning discussions, or related governance purposes. Administrative access is restricted, role-based, and logged.

### 3.5 Organizational Reporting and Role-Based Visibility

The Practice may review team-level, office-level, and organizational btyARENA information for the purpose of evaluating training effectiveness, supporting leadership development, understanding organizational learning patterns, and improving developmental programs. Access to identified information is governed through the role-based permissions described in Section 3.4 of this addendum.

### 3.6 Authentication and Account Privacy

btyARENA access is provided through sanctioned single sign-on (SSO) only. Employees are responsible for the security of their own credentials and must not share account access. Account activity (sign-in events, session records) is logged for security purposes.

### 3.7 Data Retention

The Practice retains btyARENA data for periods consistent with legitimate business, developmental, and legal purposes. Specific retention schedules will be maintained by the Practice and made available upon reasonable request.

### 3.8 Employee Access to Own Records

Employees may request to review their own btyARENA training records, subject to reasonable verification and consistent with applicable law. Requests should be made through normal Practice channels.

## 4. Conduct While Using btyARENA

### 4.1 Professional Standards

Employees are expected to engage with btyARENA in a manner consistent with the Practice's standards of professional conduct. This includes:

- Engaging honestly with scenario reflections and action records
- Refraining from entering content that is harassing, discriminatory, retaliatory, defamatory, threatening, or otherwise inconsistent with Practice policy
- Refraining from entering PHI, patient identifiers, or confidential third-party information
- Refraining from impersonating, mocking, or targeting specific coworkers in reflection content
- Refraining from sharing btyARENA content, scenarios, or other employees' information outside the Practice

### 4.2 Reporting Concerns

Employees who encounter content, scenario design, or platform behavior they believe is inappropriate, harmful, or in conflict with Practice values are encouraged to report it through normal channels.

### 4.3 Anti-Retaliation

The Practice does not retaliate against employees for raising good-faith concerns about btyARENA content, design, or use. This applies whether the concern is raised informally, through HR, or through any other channel available under Practice policy or applicable law.

## 5. Technical Matters

### 5.1 Account Provisioning

btyARENA accounts are provisioned by the Practice. Employees do not create independent accounts. Use of btyARENA through non-sanctioned accounts is prohibited.

### 5.2 Sign-Out and Shared Devices

Employees are responsible for signing out of btyARENA when leaving a shared device unattended.

### 5.3 Reporting Technical Issues and Suspected Security Events

Technical issues, suspected unauthorized access, lost devices, or other security concerns should be reported promptly through normal Practice channels.

### 5.4 Off-Boarding

Upon separation from bty DENTAL, employee btyARENA access is terminated. Retention of training records following separation is governed by the Practice's retention practices and applicable law.
