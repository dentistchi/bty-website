## Section A — Axis ↔ Arena Hidden Stats Relationship

### Purpose

This section clarifies the relationship between the 12-Axis Canon and the Arena Hidden Stats subsystem.

### Canon Statement

The 12 Core Axes are not direct measurements.

They are leadership-pattern interpretations derived from multiple upstream signals.

Arena Hidden Stats are one of those upstream signal systems.

The Hidden Stats subsystem and the Axis subsystem serve different purposes and must not be treated as equivalent layers.

Hidden Stats represent measured tendencies within the Arena environment.

Axes represent recurring decision-pattern structures used by the BTY leadership engine.

The relationship between them is directional rather than symmetrical.

Changes in certain Hidden Stats may contribute to axis-derived calculations, but an Axis is not defined by any single Hidden Stat.

Likewise, a Hidden Stat must not be interpreted as a direct proxy for an Axis.

Not all Hidden Stats participate in axis-derived calculations in the same way.

Different Hidden Stats may influence axis-derived calculations through different upstream routes and relationships.

Some participate in the current axis-calculation chain, while others remain available to the product without participating in that chain.

Therefore:

Axis ≠ Hidden Stat

The Axis layer exists above the Hidden Stat layer and may aggregate, interpret, or derive meaning from upstream signals without being reducible to them.

## Section B — Axis ↔ Emotional Stats Relationship

### Purpose

This section clarifies the relationship between the 12-Axis Canon and the Emotional Stats subsystem.

### Canon Statement

The Emotional Stats subsystem is distinct from the Axis subsystem.

The two systems serve different purposes and operate as separate layers within the current BTY implementation.

Emotional Stats are designed to observe and represent emotional-state patterns.

Axes are designed to interpret recurring leadership decision patterns.

Because these systems address different questions, they should not be treated as interchangeable measures.

An Emotional Stat is not an Axis.

Likewise, an Axis should not be interpreted as an Emotional Stat.

Within the current implementation, the Axis subsystem does not derive its meaning from Emotional Stats, and Emotional Stats do not participate in the current axis-calculation chain.

Therefore:

Axis ≠ Emotional Stat

The Emotional Stats subsystem and the Axis subsystem should be understood as parallel systems that provide different perspectives on user development.

## Section C — Integrity Terminology Disclosure

### Purpose

This section discloses the existence of multiple uses of the term “Integrity” within the BTY system.

### Canon Statement

The term “Integrity” is currently used in more than one context within BTY.

These uses should not be assumed to share the same definition simply because they share the same label.

Within the current implementation, the Integrity Hidden Stat and the Integrity Axis exist as distinct concepts.

They belong to different layers of the system and serve different purposes.

As a result, references to “Integrity” should always be interpreted within their specific context rather than by label alone.

The existence of a relationship between these concepts does not make them identical.

Likewise, a shared label should not be interpreted as evidence of a shared definition.

This addendum does not redefine either use of the term.

It does not establish priority between them.

It does not replace one definition with another.

Its purpose is solely to disclose the existence of multiple Integrity concepts and to reduce ambiguity when interpreting BTY documentation, measurements, and leadership models.

Therefore:

Shared label ≠ Shared definition
