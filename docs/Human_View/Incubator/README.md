# Human View — Incubator

Status: ACTIVE
Authority: NONE
Layer: pre-canon

## What this is

This directory holds Founder insight that is **not yet** BTY product philosophy.
Material here is thinking-in-progress, not doctrine.

## Hard rules

1. Nothing in Incubator may be cited as a product basis.
2. Nothing in Incubator carries authority.
3. Promotion to Canon requires Boundary Review. There is no automatic path.

## Promotion path

    Founder Insight
        ↓
    Boundary Review
        ↓
    Human View / Canon

This mirrors BTY's own loop: nothing becomes Growth without Verification.
A reconstructed or unverified idea is never authority before ratification.

## Boundary

Founder Root — theology, testimony, personal covenant — does NOT belong here.
That material is permanently unrecorded and lives outside this repository.
Incubator is for ideas that may one day become product philosophy.
It is not a record of the Founder's private foundation.
